{{ disclaimer }} Porchlight fills in a draft from the tax ledger to help the treasurer and your tax preparer. It doesn't file anything.

Most HOAs can choose each year to file Form 1120-H, which taxes only income that isn't from members' dues and assessments (for example bank interest), at a flat rate. The worksheet checks the qualification tests and adds up the lines.
