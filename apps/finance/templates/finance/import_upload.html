Tips

  • Download transactions from your bank's website. OFX/QFX ("Quicken" or "Money") files are best because each transaction has a unique ID.
  • Banks often limit each download to about 90 days. Import as many files as you need; overlapping days are skipped automatically.
  • Stick with one format per account. Mixing CSV and OFX for the same days can create duplicates.
  • Porchlight only reads the files you upload. It never asks for, or stores, your bank login.
